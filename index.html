About

I'm a Research Assistant at ELITE Research Lab and a Web & Automation Engineer at Cleaning Connected. I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology (AUST).

My research builds efficient, reliable deep learning for healthcare and vision — lightweight segmentation networks, privileged-information distillation, and parameter-efficient adaptation of foundation models. Through NIMISHES Lab, which I co-lead, I collaborate with researchers at the University of South Florida and the American University of Sharjah.

Interests: medical image analysis · efficient deep learning · foundation-model adaptation · reliable vision-language systems
