ELITE Research Lab LLC

Queens, NY, USA · Remote · Part-time
Research Assistant — Aug 2026 – Present
Research Intern — Jun 2026 – Aug 2026

Project: LCM-UNet — Lightweight Skin Lesion Segmentation using a Mamba-based U-Net

  • Ultra-light skin lesion segmentation network (33,474 parameters, 0.197 GMACs per 256×256 image) that restricts bidirectional Mamba to stages at one-sixteenth of the input resolution or coarser and uses local convolutions at finer scales, aimed at resource-constrained deployment.
  • Main contribution: a multi-basis reparameterizable adapter (MBRA) that trains dilated, horizontal, and vertical depthwise kernels alongside each local branch, then folds them into the host kernel via structural reparameterization before inference, adding no deployed parameters or operations.
  • Evaluated against eight retrained baselines over three seeds: highest Dice on the ISIC 2017 and ISIC 2018 test sets (88.62%, 89.73%) and second-highest on external PH2 (90.46%).
  • Promoted from Research Intern to Research Assistant in recognition of research contributions.
