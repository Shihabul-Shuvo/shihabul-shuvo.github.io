AquaProbe: Efficient Adaptation of Frozen Vision Models for Underwater Images

Accepted

First author. Authors: Md Shihabul Islam Shovo, Nishi Kanta Paul, Trung Dong, Xiaomin Lin (NIMISHES Lab · University of South Florida)

5th Workshop on Maritime Computer Vision (MaCVi), IEEE/CVF Winter Conference on Applications of Computer Vision (WACV 2027)

A degradation-conditioned probe that measures where the underwater domain gap grows inside a frozen vision encoder, then splits a fixed LoRA rank budget across blocks in proportion to that measured profile. Clean RGB-D scenes are physically re-rendered across 10 Jerlov water types and 4 imaging ranges, and per-block CKA divergence (rather than the usual cumulative curve) shows where new divergence is introduced.

  • 70.1 mIoU on SUIM with profile-proportional LoRA allocation (DINOv2 ViT-B/14) — +1.9 mIoU over uniform allocation at the same 0.59M adapter-parameter budget
  • Uniform allocation needs 4x the adapter parameters to match it
  • Also +1.5 mIoU on SUIM with SAM ViT-B and +1.5 mIoU on TrashCan at equal budget
  • Condition-routed variant: 70.6 mIoU on SUIM and 63.4 AUDRC under a held-out degradation range

WaveFoG: Wavelet-Gated Transformer for Parkinson's Freezing of Gait Detection Using Wearable Accelerometer Signals

Accepted

First author. Authors: Md Shihabul Islam Shovo, Nishi Kanta Paul, Adrita Rahman, Israt Jerin Esha

5th IEEE Int. Conf. on Biomedical Engineering, Computer and Information Technology for Health (BECITHCON 2026), Dhaka, Bangladesh, 4–5 September 2026

A dual-branch CNN-Transformer that fuses discrete wavelet sub-band descriptors through a sigmoid gating layer to detect freezing-of-gait episodes from wrist-worn accelerometers under severe class imbalance.

  • F1 0.875 ± 0.017 and AUPRC 0.833 ± 0.020 on the Kaggle TLVMC FoG dataset
  • Subject-independent grouped ten-fold cross-validation
  • Up to +5.4pp F1 over 1D CNN, Bi-LSTM, and vanilla Transformer baselines
  • Temporal saliency maps align with reported FoG onset characteristics

SleepEffFormer: Efficient CNN-Transformer with Transition-Aware Smoothing for Single-Channel EEG Sleep Stage Classification

Accepted

Authors: Nishi Kanta Paul, Md Shihabul Islam Shovo, Israt Jerin Esha, Adrita Rahman

5th IEEE BECITHCON 2026, Dhaka, Bangladesh, 4–5 September 2026

A lightweight CNN-Transformer for automated sleep staging from a single EEG channel, paired with a non-parametric Transition-Aware Smoothing (TAS) layer that suppresses physiologically implausible stage transitions.

  • 83.9% accuracy, 78.9% macro-F1, Cohen's kappa 0.765 on Sleep-EDF Expanded (78 all-night recordings, subject-wise split)
  • Matches AttnSleep with 3–5x fewer parameters (~367K)
  • Transformer encoder adds +6.6pp macro-F1; TAS adds +1.8pp with zero trainable parameters
  • Attention maps recover physiologically meaningful sleep-stage EEG signatures

DR-LiteNet: A Lightweight Explainable Hybrid CNN Framework for Imbalanced Diabetic Retinopathy Grading via Adaptive SMOTE Fusion

Accepted

Authors: Nishi Kanta Paul, Md Shihabul Islam Shovo, Audrija Chowdhury, Israt Jahan Esha

1st IEEE Int. Conf. on Next-Generation Electrical & Electronics, Computer Systems, and Technologies (iCONEECT 2026)

A dual-branch network that fuses 1,280-d EfficientNetB0 features with a 51-d handcrafted lesion descriptor (vessel density, exudate intensity, texture entropy, HSV histograms), then applies ADASYN in feature space to correct five-grade class imbalance without touching the images.

  • 88.77% ± 0.61 accuracy, QWK 0.884 ± 0.010, macro-F1 0.792 ± 0.008 on APTOS 2019
  • Only 4.69M parameters — beats ResNet50 (23.5M) by ΔQWK = 0.022
  • Two-phase training: balanced head warm-up, then end-to-end fine-tuning
  • Grad-CAM saliency maps for post-hoc clinical explainability

LCM-UNet: Scale-Selective Mamba with Reparameterizable Multi-Basis Local Modeling for Lightweight Skin Lesion Segmentation

Under review

First author (equal contribution with Nishi Kanta Paul). Authors: Md Shihabul Islam Shovo, Nishi Kanta Paul, Dip Kumar Saha, Kishor Morol

IEEE Journal of Biomedical and Health Informatics (JBHI) — Q1, IF 7.7

Allocates global and local modeling by spatial scale: bidirectional Mamba runs only at stages at one-sixteenth of the input resolution or coarser, while a multi-basis reparameterizable adapter (MBRA) trains dilated, horizontal, and vertical depthwise kernels alongside each local branch and folds them into the host kernel before inference.

  • 33,474 deployed parameters and 0.197 GMACs per 256×256 image
  • Highest Dice among nine networks retrained under a harmonized protocol: 88.62% ± 0.21 on ISIC 2017 and 89.73% ± 0.18 on ISIC 2018 (three seeds)
  • External validation on PH2: 90.46% ± 0.31 Dice, second among the compared models
  • The adapter improves Dice by 0.48pp over a single host kernel with no added deployed parameters or operations; its reparameterization is exact up to floating-point rounding
  • Removing Mamba from the one-eighth-resolution decoder stage eliminates 63% of Mamba operations with a Dice change of −0.03pp

APED-CXR: Adaptive Privileged Evidence Distillation for Chest X-Ray Grounding with Image-Only Inference

Under review

First author. Authors: Md Shihabul Islam Shovo, Nishi Kanta Paul, Jamil Fayyad (NIMISHES Lab · American University of Sharjah)

IEEE International Symposium on Biomedical Imaging (ISBI) 2027 — submitted; preprint arXiv:2610.29740, 21 October 2026

An 11.1M-parameter student learns report-informed spatial evidence from a frozen multimodal teacher. During training, the teacher verifies regions proposed from the student's current evidence maps using the image, report, and queried class. The student predicts disease labels and bounding boxes using only the image at inference.

  • PadChest-GR box-level micro-gF1: 0.338 ± 0.018 versus 0.302 ± 0.005 with fixed warm-up proposals (IoU ≥ 0.5); paired gain 0.036, 95% CI [0.022, 0.049], across five seeds
  • Diagnostic macro-AUC 0.815; NIH ChestX-ray14 secondary box-recall check: 0.447 versus 0.423
  • 11.1M parameters, 5.7 GMACs, and 9.6 ms per image on an A100 (batch size 1), including box extraction
  • Teacher and reports are training-only; adaptive supervision costs 34.4 teacher GPU-hours per seed versus 5.9 for fixed proposals. NIH is not independent external validation because it was used to train the teacher.

Band2Net: Frequency-to-Network Transport Distillation for fMRI-Privileged, EEG-Only Session-Condition Decoding

Under review

Authors: Nishi Kanta Paul, Md Shihabul Islam Shovo, Dwarikanath Mahapatra

IEEE International Symposium on Biomedical Imaging (ISBI) 2027

Treats fMRI as privileged information available only during training. EEG is encoded into five frequency-specific connectivity graphs and fMRI into seven canonical network tokens from a frozen teacher; a balanced entropic transport problem between the two token sets gives each EEG band its own fMRI target.

  • 0.801 ROC-AUC for EEG-only session-condition decoding on the PsiConnect psilocybin dataset, under subject-grouped cross-validation
  • 5.0 AUC points (95% CI 1.8–8.2) above an EEG-only student
  • EEG and fMRI come from the same participants under matched conditions, not simultaneously; inference uses EEG only
  • Margins over a single shared plan, a KL-relaxed plan, and label-free network descriptors remain unresolved

LAPIS: Clinically Constrained Patient-Identity Residual Learning for Chest X-Ray Anonymization

Under review

Authors: Nishi Kanta Paul, Md Shihabul Islam Shovo, Désiré Sidibé

IEEE International Symposium on Biomedical Imaging (ISBI) 2027

Adapts a spatial chest-radiograph latent space so that patient-discriminative information concentrates in a compact, replaceable channel residual while most clinically relevant content stays in the complement. At release, the residual is replaced by a mixture of donor residuals.

  • Reduces worst-case cross-study linkability from 0.961 to 0.791 AUC at a macro AUROC cost of 0.012 relative to reconstruction
  • The residual is defined by an exactly semi-orthogonal basis, and a spatial adversary suppresses identity leakage from the complement
  • Replacement-aware training teaches the decoder cross-patient residual swaps under output identity, clinical, and realism constraints
  • Evaluated on patient-disjoint ChestX-ray14 sets with adapted attackers and a structural source-retrieval attack; a deformation-based baseline remains better at structural source retrieval

Coupled or Independent? Fault Coupling in Agentic Medical Vision Systems

Under review

Authors: Nishi Kanta Paul, Md Shihabul Islam Shovo, Md Mostafijur Rahman, Jamil Fayyad

Workshop on Foundation AI for Biomedical Reasoning, Imaging, and Cognition (FABRIC), IEEE/CVF Winter Conference on Applications of Computer Vision (WACV 2027)

Tests whether tool-using vision-language agents are actually safer than single-shot models when the tool channel (segmentation and contrastive retrieval) and the reasoning channel (an iterative VLM reasoner) share one visual fault. Introduces a coupling taxonomy and proves that, under a binary decision, a hard-label consistency check can never catch joint failures.

  • Dermatology agent evaluated on PAD-UFES-20 and an independent external HIBA test set, with two open-weight 7B reasoners (Qwen2.5-VL, LLaVA-Med) and three contrastive encoders
  • Typographic prompt injection produces detectable directional retrieval capture; physical corruption does not
  • Pipeline order alone decides the failure type: crop-first retrieval gives 55% joint wrong agreement vs. 35% for full-image retrieval
  • Disagreement recall limited to 0.29 (crop-first) vs. 0.57 (full-image); effect reproduced on the external test set
